Each line of standard input is a string of characters. For each line print `ok` if its brackets ()[]{} are balanced and properly nested (other characters are ignored), otherwise print `error at N`, where N is the 1-based position (in bytes) of the first character that makes it unbalanced: a closing bracket that doesn't match the innermost open one or has nothing to close; or, if the line ends with brackets still open, N is the line's length plus 1. Lines are separated by `\n`; a final newline doesn't start another line.
