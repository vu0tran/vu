Standard input is CSV with a header line. Columns are separated by commas; a field may be quoted with double quotes, in which case it can contain commas, and a doubled quote `""` inside it stands for one quote. The header names the columns; one of them is named `region` and another `amount` (in any position). `amount` is a decimal number with at most 2 digits after the point (it may be negative). Print, for each distinct region in order of first appearance, a line `REGION TOTAL` where TOTAL is the sum of its amounts printed with exactly 2 digits after the point (e.g. `-3.50`). Lines end with `\n`; there are no line breaks inside fields.
