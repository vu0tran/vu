Each line of standard input is an arithmetic expression over 64-bit signed integers with + - * / (integer division truncating toward zero), unary minus, and parentheses; spaces may appear anywhere. * and / bind tighter than + and -; operators of equal precedence group left to right. For each non-empty line print its value, or `error` if the expression is malformed (anything but digits, spaces, + - * / and parentheses is malformed too) or divides by zero; empty lines are skipped. Every intermediate value fits in 64 bits.
