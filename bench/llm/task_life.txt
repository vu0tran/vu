Conway's Game of Life on a torus. Standard input: a first line `H W T`, then H lines of W characters each, `#` (alive) or `.` (dead). Run T generations (0 <= T <= 1000) where the grid wraps around at the edges (row -1 is row H-1, etc.), then print the grid in the same format. A live cell with 2 or 3 live neighbours stays alive; a dead cell with exactly 3 becomes alive; every other cell is dead. 1 <= H, W <= 100.
