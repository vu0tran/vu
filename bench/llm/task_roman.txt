Each non-empty line of standard input is a decimal integer, a Roman numeral, or something else (empty lines are skipped). For a number print its Roman numeral (standard form: I V X L C D M with subtractive pairs IV IX XL XC CD CM); for a Roman numeral in standard form print its value; for anything else (including non-standard numerals such as IIII or VX, lowercase letters, numbers with leading zeros or a sign, and numbers outside 1..3999) print `invalid`. Print one line per input line.
